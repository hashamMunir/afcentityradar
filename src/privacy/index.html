What we process and why

  • Account data (email, password hash, chosen use type, plan): to provide your account — contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Stored with Supabase in the EU region we select.
  • Usage counters: number of checks per account, or per anonymised connection (a daily-rotating, irreversible hash of the IP address): to enforce free limits and prevent abuse — legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Deleted after 120 days.
  • Screened names are never stored or logged. They are processed in memory to return the result.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see card details.
  • Security logs kept by our hosting provider Netlify for a short period.

Public data about screened people

Results come from official lists and public sources for anti-money-laundering, counter-terrorist-financing and fraud prevention purposes. If you believe information about you is wrong, contact us and the original source; we will review it.
