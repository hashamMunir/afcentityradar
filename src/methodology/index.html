Methodology and risk scoring

Name matching

Names are normalised (accents, titles and legal forms removed), compared token by token with Jaro-Winkler similarity and phonetic keys (so Shehbaz, Shahbaz and Shabaz match), with low weight for common name parts. Matches of 92% or more are strong; 84–92% are possible and count half.

Limitations
