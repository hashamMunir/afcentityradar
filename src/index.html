Free AML, sanctions and PEP screening in one search

AFC Entity Radar is an open-source-intelligence (OSINT) screening tool for anti-financial-crime, KYC and compliance teams. Enter a person or company and get, in seconds, potential matches on official sanctions and watchlists, politically exposed person (PEP) status with relatives and close associates, offshore-leaks appearances, adverse media, country risk and a transparent 0–100 risk score with every source linked.

Sanctions & watchlists

UN, US OFAC, EU, UK, Swiss, Canadian and Australian sanctions, terrorist and proscribed lists, Interpol red notices, FBI and NCA most-wanted, World Bank debarment.

PEP & relationship network

Political positions, family and business links two steps out, each node checked against the lists and the offshore leaks.

Fewer false positives

Spelling-variant matching, low weight for common name parts, and birth year and country checks that automatically down-weight records that do not fit.

Offshore leaks & adverse media

Panama, Paradise and Pandora Papers via ICIJ; corruption, fraud and sanctions mentions highlighted from public sources.

Country risk

FATF black and grey lists, EU high-risk third countries and Basel AML Index on an interactive globe.
